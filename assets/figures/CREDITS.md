## Connected neighbourhood story

The complete technical readings additionally preserve all source-note illustrations and their original adjacent attribution. The six source workflow diagrams have separate rebuild notes below.

These small coordinates are drawing offsets, not the full UTM coordinates of surveyed features. The park measures `80 m × 60 m`, an area of `4800 m²`.

## Phase 1–4 step visuals

The 126 `phase1-<chapter>-step-N.svg` through `phase4-<chapter>-step-N.svg` assets are original teaching illustrations defined in [`early-steps.js`](../../early-steps.js). Build with `npm run build:early-steps`. The builder reuses [`scripts/phase56-figures.cjs`](../../scripts/phase56-figures.cjs), preserves existing Phase 1 step illustrations, and checks coverage of all 147 early-phase reading steps.

- Phase 1 uses the same invented school, road and population story. Plots labelled relative to School A shift the local origin without moving the features. The 60/80 m road legs, 100 m direct distance, 200 m selection rule and 200-person union are the existing teaching checkpoints. Terrain and uncertainty diagrams are explicitly illustrative; uncertainty intervals are a bounded-error example, not confidence intervals.
- Phase 2 uses the documented Amsterdam parcel coordinates, DEM extent/cell counts, tile addresses and binary-layout examples. The MVT square is plotted with **negative tile y** to show the format’s screen-down axis; its actual coordinates remain `(10,10)` through `(30,30)`. Diagrammed HTTP, metadata and release workflows are contracts, not evidence of live service execution.
- Phase 3’s flood overlay uses the actual synthetic rectangle coordinates on equal metre scales, offset from UTM `(500000,5700000)`. Population, raster and detour charts show the supplied checkpoints. The three-node Dijkstra table and unequal-block mean example are separate, clearly labelled arithmetic illustrations.
- Phase 4’s district plot uses the actual synthetic EPSG:28992 fixture with offsets `(119000,486000)` on equal-scale axes. Incident I2 lies on the shared district boundary. Radius counts, network costs and valid-time results come from the course’s hand-checkable fixtures. Workflow and comparison diagrams are not query-plan benchmarks or database execution logs.

Every new SVG includes a title and description; its in-app caption explains assumptions and units. Text labels accompany colours. All original source-note figures and existing chapter overview diagrams retain their original provenance.

## Original Fieldnotes study diagrams

Twelve local `study-*.svg` illustrations are generated by [`scripts/build-study-figures.cjs`](../../scripts/build-study-figures.cjs). They use explicit coordinates and the existing [`geo.js`](../../geo.js) reference implementation, not generated photographs or invented geographic outlines. Their `<title>` and `<desc>` elements describe the diagrams, and scale assumptions are printed inside each image. These are original project illustrations, not reproductions of third-party artwork.

Rebuild from the repository root with `node scripts/build-study-figures.cjs`. No additional dependencies are required.

| File | Basis / how to read the geometry |
|---|---|
| `study-heights.svg` | Local height-reference schematic, h ≈ H + N; synthetic 50, 30 and 20 m values. Vertical separation exaggerated and distinct vertical directions simplified. |
| `study-axis-order.svg` | Exact Amsterdam and swapped coordinate pairs plotted on an angular graticule; no claim of distance preservation. |
| `study-mercator.svg` | Equal infinitesimal ground squares with sides scaled by spherical sec(latitude), areas by its square. Common drawing scale. |
| `study-transform.svg` | WGS 84 sixth-order Krüger UTM calculation from `Geo.utm`; same point, different units and coordinates. |
| `study-degree-lengths.svg` | WGS 84 local meridional and prime-vertical curvature from `Geo.degreeLengths`; common-scale bars. |
| `study-cell-centres.svg` | Source-note north-up affine example, origin (570000, 5820000), pixel size (+2000, −2000) m. |
| `study-bilinear.svg` | Source-note samples 10, 20, 30, 40; u=0.25, v=0.5; explicitly computed weights and `Geo.bilinear`. |
| `study-accuracy.svg` | Symmetric synthetic observations; bias and spread varied independently on identical targets. |
| `study-classification.svg` | Source-note density values and `Geo.breaks`: equal interval, quantile and Fisher-Jenks. Values and ordering stay fixed. |
| `study-predicates.svg` | Explicit planar rectangles: disjoint, shared edge, shared area, strict containment. |
| `study-coverage-union.svg` | Synthetic nine-cell masks sharing two cells; 10 people/cell; 16 distinct selected cells. Not operational catchment data. |
| `study-vector-raster.svg` | Identical circle extent; raster cells selected with an exact centre-inside-circle test at two resolutions. |

Formula explanations and assumptions are in the corresponding sections of [`core-geospatial-concepts.md`](../../core-geospatial-concepts.md). Height terminology follows the [NOAA National Geodetic Survey geoid explanation](https://geodesy.noaa.gov/GEOID/geoid_def.html); affine corner/centre conventions follow the [GDAL geotransform tutorial](https://gdal.org/en/stable/tutorials/geotransforms_tut.html).
